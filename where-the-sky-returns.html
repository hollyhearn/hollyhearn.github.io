02 / Lake Chagan, Kazakhstan

Where the
Sky Returns

Last Nuclear Bomb Memorial Competition

Project
Last Nuclear Bomb Memorial Competition / Buildner
Location
Lake Chagan, eastern Kazakhstan
Focus
Memory, walking, and the landscape
Tools
Rhino, Photoshop, Illustrator, ArcGIS

A journey from the enduring fear of the nuclear age toward openness, relief, and the expansive sky.

Where the Sky Returns imagines a future in which the final nuclear weapon has been dismantled. The memorial remembers the fear of the nuclear age while celebrating its end.

The proposal is sited at Lake Chagan, a reservoir formed by a Soviet nuclear test in 1965. The crater becomes the setting for an experience that confronts the legacy of destruction before gradually opening toward the surrounding landscape.

Through the slowness of walking, the project reveals both the scale of the scar and the hope that we may one day leave this era behind.
