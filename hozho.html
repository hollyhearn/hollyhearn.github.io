Hozho Voices of
Healing Center

Connecting with nature, community, and self

Project
LA 503 / Indigenous Design Studio
Location
Outside Crownpoint, New Mexico
Focus
Connection, healing, and education
Tools
AutoCAD, Illustrator, Photoshop, QGIS, Rhino

A footpath connecting the land, the community, and the self.

Developed in a studio working with Hozho Voices of Healing Center, this proposal reimagines connections across a former trading post in the checkerboard region of Dinétah. A continuous path links existing structures, traditional Diné ways of life, and classmates’ design proposals.

The path addresses drainage and erosion while creating space for mind-body healing, education, and everyday connection. Cardinal directions, sight lines, and existing structures such as the Hogan inform its alignment.

Three stages move from public encounters with nature to spaces for community gathering, and finally toward quieter, more private places for reflection.

The connecting footpath

Studio collaboration: the overall plan includes classmates’ proposals. Credits in the source portfolio include Jonathan Admire, Maria Lagunas, Elisha Tamrakar, Katherine Santistevan, Lashannon Begay, Leah Caminite, and Kailani Gorman.
